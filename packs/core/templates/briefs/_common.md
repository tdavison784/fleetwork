# Preamble: every lane reads this first

- Read `FLEET.md` (binding), then `MISSION.md`, then the `rules/` files, then your brief.
- Your ledger item says which profile you run under and what is guarded (`ledger` view: `LEDGER.yaml`). You
  never edit the ledger.
- Done = your brief's "Done when" holds, your checks pass, `status/<lane>.md` is current, you have cleaned up,
  and your final message starts with `LANE DONE:`.
